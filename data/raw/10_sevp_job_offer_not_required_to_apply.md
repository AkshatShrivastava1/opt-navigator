# Do You Need a Job Offer or Employer to Apply for OPT? — DHS Study in the States (SEVIS Help Hub)
Source: https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/sevis-help-hub/student-records/fm-student-employment/f-1-optional-practical-training-opt
Publisher: DHS / SEVP
Last updated: May 19, 2025

## Job offer / employer is not required to apply for OPT

While students are not required to have an employer when OPT is requested in SEVIS, students authorized for post-completion OPT are expected to be employed. A student on post-completion OPT can be unemployed for a total of 90 days.

In other words, a student does not need a job offer or an employer lined up in order to apply for OPT. Eligibility to apply is based on the student's academic enrollment and status, the Designated School Official's (DSO) recommendation of OPT in SEVIS, and a properly filed Form I-765, not on having secured employment. Employment matters after OPT is approved: a student authorized for post-completion OPT is expected to be employed and may accrue no more than 90 days of unemployment (150 days total with the STEM OPT extension).

## Note on the STEM OPT extension

The STEM OPT extension is different: it requires a paid training opportunity with an employer enrolled in E-Verify, so an employer is required for the STEM extension even though one is not required to apply for initial OPT.
